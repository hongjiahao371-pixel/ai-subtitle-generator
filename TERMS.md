# AI 字幕生成器 用户协议 / User Agreement

**生效及更新日期 / Effective and last updated: 2026-10-25**

## 1. 服务说明 / Service Description

AI 字幕生成器是开源的本地字幕工具：基于 Whisper 离线语音识别为视频生成时间轴字幕，支持双语烧录、离线/本地大模型翻译、OCR 字幕提取、格式互转与编辑预览。全部处理默认在用户自己的设备上完成。

AI Subtitle Generator is an open-source, local subtitle tool: offline Whisper speech recognition generates timed subtitles, with bilingual burning, offline/local-LLM translation, OCR extraction, format conversion, and editing preview. Everything runs on your own device by default.

## 2. AI 生成内容说明 / AI-Generated Content Notice

字幕由本地 AI 模型自动生成，可能存在识别或翻译错误（专有名词、数字、多说话人场景尤甚）。**重要内容请人工校对后使用**，请勿将未校对的自动字幕用于法律、医疗、新闻等高准确性要求场景。

Subtitles are generated automatically by local AI models and may contain recognition or translation errors (especially proper nouns, numbers, and multi-speaker scenes). **Proofread before relying on them.** Do not use unreviewed automatic subtitles where high accuracy is required (legal, medical, news, etc.).

Process only videos/audio you own or are authorized to use, and comply with copyright law in your jurisdiction. Do not use the app to process or distribute infringing or unlawful content.

安装时选择的媒体目录为只读挂载；字幕与结果保存在应用数据目录。执行删除或卸载前请自行确认并备份重要结果。
